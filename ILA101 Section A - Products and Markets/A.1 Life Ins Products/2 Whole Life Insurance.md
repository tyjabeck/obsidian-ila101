## **Participating vs Non-Participating WL**

**Participating WL** – Allows policyholders to share in the insurer's profits through dividend payments to policyholders.

**Common Dividend Options Available to PHs:**

1. Cash  
2. Apply against premium  
3. Purchase paid-up additions and/or OYT (one-year term) insurance  

---

## **Premium Patterns**

- **Ordinary Level** (most common)  
  - Determined at issue and payable for life.  

- **Indeterminate Premium WL** – Non-par WL with annually adjustable premiums  
  - Insurer can adjust premiums to reflect experience  
  - Premiums cannot exceed predetermined guaranteed maximum rates  
  - Generally offers a lower initial premium than ordinary level premium WL  

- **Limited Payment WL** – Premiums are paid for a set number of years but provide coverage for life  

---

## **Pricing Considerations for WL**

### **Mortality**

- Lack of premium flexibility in a typical WL plan creates significant mortality risk.  
- Coverage can last many decades, so mortality assumptions are critical.

---

### **Persistency (Lapse)**

A 2012 SOA persistency study found:

- WL lapse rates have generally been declining  
- Lapses increase in poor economic conditions  
- Smaller policies lapse more in early years; larger policies lapse more in later years  
- Male and female lapses are similar  
- Less underwriting leads to higher premiums, which cause higher early lapses  

---

### **Underwriting**

- Industry is trending toward **accelerated underwriting (AUW)**
