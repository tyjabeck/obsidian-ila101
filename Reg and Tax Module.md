### Task 5
- Because the return of premium is on death, YRT would be the most applicable form of reinsurance as it 
- year-by-year YRT premium rates are calculated to cover the year-by-year mortality costs
- three reasons to reinsure
	1. retention limits - RT may have a retention limit on the benefits and reinsure the amounts in excess of their retention limits. helps mitigate the effects of 1 large policy
	2. new business - if NB is growing too fast against the company's capital, RT can reinsure and use the reinsurance premiums as new capital to fund more NB
	3. tax benefits - reinsurance can accelerate current profits for the company in exchance for a share of future profits on business already written. if the company incurred significant losses while developing the product, they usually only have a few years to use those losses for tax deductions
